# First-time login

To start using **Computerome**, you need a user account — whether you plan to use **HPC**, **Cloud**, or both.

!!! note

    If you are connecting from abroad, ip restrictions might apply to you. See [IP Restrictions](ip-restrictions.md#ip-restrictions) for more details.

## Step 1: Create Your Account

1. Go to the [**Create Account page**](https://www.computerome.dk/create-account).
2. Fill in the registration form with your information.
3. You will receive a **welcome email** with an activation link.
4. Follow the link to activate your account and set your password.

## Step 2: Activate Your 2FA Token

Once your account is activated, you must enable **two-factor authentication (2FA)** to access Computerome services.

1. Go to the [**2FA Activation Guide**](../two-factor-authentication-2fa.md).
2. Follow the steps to register your 2FA token.

!!! note

    2FA is mandatory for both HPC and Cloud users.
