# Open On Demand

OnDemand provides an integrated, single access point for all of your HPC resources

Connect to:

[Computerome OpenOnDemand Portal (https://ood.computerome.dk/)](https://ood.computerome.dk/)

Use your HPC account and password and there is currently 10 sec time limit to accept the push notification on your phone. Please have the push token present and ready to accept on PrivacyIdea while you login.

This should open the portal to our HPC resources where you can mulitple applications interactively such as Rstudio server, VSCode, interactive desktops etc.
![](../assets/uploads/OOD_de2b28a477.png)

You can also use the file manager to upload and download data from your projects directly with this portal.

![](../assets/uploads/OOD_file_Manager_ba9518f24e.png)

You can start your interactive desktop sessions and also submit your jobs via Job Composer or through Slurm Cluster Shell access under the Clusters tab.

![](../assets/uploads/Slurm_Status_ac20e1966a.png)

You can track the status of the jobs under Jobs tab on the navigation bar.
