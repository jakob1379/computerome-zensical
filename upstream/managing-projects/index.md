# Managing Projects

You can have multiple projects in **Computerome**, either **Cloud projects** or **HPC projects**.
You can also be a member of multiple projects at the same time.

You can have different **roles** in different projects. Based on the role that you have in a project, you will also have different **responsibilities**.

Before you continue, please make sure you have a very good understanding of our **Shared Responsibility Matrix**, which you can find here:
